# Community and Moderation Policy

Status: Adopted baseline

## Expected conduct

Greyfoundry spaces must support respectful technical disagreement, good-faith participation, and the safety of contributors and users. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate exposure of private information, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and retaliation for reporting are not acceptable.

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, organization-managed community spaces, events, and public representation of Greyfoundry.

## Moderation principles

- Safety and confidentiality take priority over public debate about a report.
- Enforcement is based on behavior and impact, not contribution status.
- A maintainer must not be the sole investigator of a complaint about themselves when an independent moderator is available.
- Reports, evidence, actions, and conflicts are shared only with people who need them.
- Retaliation and attempts to identify a confidential reporter are separate violations.
- Consequences should be proportionate, but credible threats or severe abuse may require immediate restriction or removal.

## Reporting

Do not put sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Use an established private contact with an organization owner or moderator. Until a dedicated private conduct channel is published, a reporter may use the affected repository's private vulnerability-reporting feature and clearly label the submission `Confidential conduct report`. This temporary route must be replaced before broad community growth.

Credible threats, stalking, or imminent danger should also be reported to the relevant platform and local authorities where appropriate.

## Process

1. Acknowledge the report and identify conflicts of interest.
2. Apply immediate protective measures when needed.
3. Gather the minimum necessary evidence and allow a fair response where safe.
4. Decide based on documented facts and policy.
5. Communicate the outcome to the reporter and affected person to the extent confidentiality allows.
6. Record the action, duration, appeal path, and follow-up date.

Possible actions include a private correction, warning, content removal, interaction limit, temporary suspension, role removal, organization removal, or permanent ban.

## Appeals

An appeal must identify a factual or procedural error, material new evidence, or disproportionality. It should be reviewed by someone who did not make the original decision when independent capacity exists. Appeals do not require exposing a reporter's identity.

## Capacity trigger

Before Greyfoundry operates a large community space, it must appoint at least two qualified moderators, publish a stable private reporting channel, define evidence retention, and test the escalation route.

# Greyfoundry Code of Conduct

Greyfoundry is committed to respectful technical disagreement, good-faith participation, and the safety of contributors and users.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not personal characteristics.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted interaction.
- Give credit, correct mistakes, and make space for newcomers.
- Follow maintainer and moderator directions intended to protect the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of private information, sustained disruption, impersonation, retaliation for reporting, and attempts to identify a confidential reporter are not acceptable.

This code applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, organization-managed spaces and events, and public representation of Greyfoundry.

## Reporting

Do not put sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Use an established private contact with an organization owner or moderator. Until a dedicated private conduct channel is published, a reporter may use the affected repository's private vulnerability-reporting feature and label the submission `Confidential conduct report`.

Credible threats, stalking, or imminent danger should also be reported to the relevant platform and local authorities where appropriate.

## Enforcement

Enforcement is based on behavior and impact, not contribution status. Possible actions include a private correction, warning, content removal, interaction limit, temporary suspension, role removal, organization removal, or permanent ban.

Reports are handled confidentially by people without a material conflict where independent capacity exists. Retaliation is a separate violation. Appeals must identify a factual or procedural error, material new evidence, or disproportionality.

The full process is defined in the [Community and Moderation Policy](docs/policies/COMMUNITY_AND_MODERATION.md).
